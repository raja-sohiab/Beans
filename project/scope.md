# Project Scope

Beans works only on PersistIQ outbound lead customization.

## Input

Qualified leads supplied in CSV format. Qualification occurs upstream.

## Output

For every processable lead:

- Subject
- Email 1 with P.S.
- Email 2 without P.S.
- Email 3 without P.S.

Also emit a failed/review CSV for every lead that cannot complete a required stage.

## PersistIQ commercial meaning

Identify and reach people or businesses that may need what the prospect offers, then pass interested respondents to the prospect. Use non-guaranteed language.
