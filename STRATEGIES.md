# Email 1 Strategy Registry

## Strategy selection principle

The strategy is selected from evidence, not distributed for variety. Variation must come from the lead's actual offering, beneficiary, problem, confidence, and commercial situation.

## `direct_offer`

### Purpose

Directly connect a supported customer need to the offer to find and reach relevant potential customers and pass interested respondents to the prospect.

### Use when

- The offering or benefit is clear.
- A beneficiary or broad beneficiary is supported.
- A direct commercial proposition is more honest than a speculative framing.

### Do not use when

- The service cannot be understood at all.
- The beneficiary would be fabricated.
- The direct wording would imply current intent or guaranteed demand.

## `referral_hypothesis`

### Purpose

Carefully hypothesize that referrals, word of mouth, networks, or existing channels may not reach every relevant prospect, then offer outbound reach as an additional channel.

### Use when

- Website evidence mentions referrals, partnerships, networks, reputation, or relationship-driven acquisition.
- The hypothesis can be explicitly softened.

### Do not use when

- There is no acquisition evidence.
- The message would assert the prospect relies on referrals as fact.

## `pain_problem`

### Purpose

Frame a supported category-level customer problem that the prospect solves, then connect buyer discovery to the PersistIQ offer.

### Use when

- The website clearly describes a problem, risk, inefficiency, or desired outcome.
- The problem can be stated without pretending to know the prospect's customers' current situation.

### Do not use when

- The pain is only a generic assumption.
- The message would manufacture urgency or consequences.

## `qualification_uncertainty`

### Purpose

Use conservative wording when the service is understandable but the beneficiary, use case, or commercial fit is broad or uncertain.

### Use when

- Stage 2 is partial or low confidence.
- A correction or qualification question is more honest than a specific claim.

### Do not use when

- A clearer direct offer is strongly supported and qualification wording would be needlessly vague.

## Selection output

Every selection must contain:

- One strategy ID
- Selection reason
- Confidence
- Supporting evidence IDs
- Compatible component IDs
- Claims to avoid
- A semantic generation brief

The brief must not be a finished email or reusable sentence frame.
